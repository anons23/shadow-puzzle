# Shadows — CrazyGames submission

**Title:** Shadows
**Category:** Puzzle (tags: puzzle, logic, 3D, relaxing, brain, casual)
**Orientation:** portrait game, works in landscape on desktop (controls move to the side)
**Build:** `python3 tools/build_crazygames.py shadows` → `dist/shadows-crazygames.zip` (upload the zip)

## Description
A candle, a wooden object and an outline on the wall. Rotate the object until its shadow fits the outline perfectly.

Shadows is a calm 3D puzzle about light and form. Every level is a little riddle: a chair turns into a house, a cup into a key — but only from the right angle. Turn, tilt and roll the object, watch the shadow change in real time and catch the moment it clicks into place.

- 60 handcrafted levels in four rooms: Attic, Nursery, Theatre and Workshop
- A new Daily Puzzle every day with a streak to keep
- Objects made of two and three parts, a third rotation axis in later levels
- Hints if you get stuck, three stars for quick solutions
- Cozy candlelit atmosphere and calm generated music

## Controls
- Mouse / touch: drag across the scene to rotate the object (left–right turns it, up–down tilts it)
- Slider (later levels): roll the object
- Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to rotate, Q / E to roll
